Pricing & Coverage

How much does a truck or cargo van wrap cost?

Typical partial wraps run $1,800–$3,200, and full vehicle wraps run $3,800–$6,500. Vehicle size, coverage, materials, design, and installation affect the final quote.

Getting Started

What do you need for a truck or cargo van wrap quote?

Share the vehicle year, make and model, photos, the coverage you want, your logo or artwork, deadline, and installation location. Your service list and phone number help us plan a readable layout.

Design & Production

Can Toughjobs handle wrap design and production?

The scope can include message planning, design, proof preparation, vinyl production, and installation coordination. Your quote will identify the services, materials, and installation costs included.

Proofing & Installation

What should we check before the wrap goes to production?

Review spelling, phone numbers, URLs, and brand details. Confirm the vehicle template, measurements, photos, coverage level, production files, and installer requirements before approving the proof.
