Pricing & Costs

How much does custom branded apparel cost?

Pricing runs by piece and method. Embroidered polos (minimum 12) run $28–$45/shirt, screen-printed t-shirts (minimum 24) run $14–$22/shirt, embroidered hoodies and jackets run $45–$110/piece, and a hi-vis workwear program runs $28–$65/piece. A full crew launch for 10 techs head-to-toe — polos, tees, hoodies, hats, hi-vis, jackets, logo digitized, all sizes stocked — runs $4,500–$9,000 one-time.

What factors determine the price per item?

Decoration method (embroidery costs more than screen print but holds up better on polos and jackets), garment weight and quality, and order quantity all move the price. We match the method to the garment rather than using the cheapest method across the board.

Is artwork setup or digitization included?

Yes. Your logo is digitized once, professionally, and the file is stored for every future run — you don't pay a setup fee again on reorders.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Yes, but kept reasonable — embroidered polos start at a minimum of 12, screen-printed tees at a minimum of 24. We won't force a 100-piece order on a 6-person shop.

Our Process

How do you recommend screen printing, embroidery, heat transfer, or another method?

Embroidery for polos, jackets, and hats — premium look, durable, ideal for logos. Screen printing for high-volume t-shirts and hoodies — sharp color, cost-effective at scale. DTF transfers for smaller runs, complex color, and hi-vis where embroidery would compromise reflectivity. We pick the right method per garment, not the cheapest one across the board.

Materials, Sizes & Production

What is the difference between screen printing, embroidery, and heat transfer?

Embroidery gives a premium, durable look best for polos, jackets, and hats. Screen printing gives sharp color and is cost-effective at scale for high-volume t-shirts and hoodies. DTF (direct-to-film) transfers suit smaller runs, complex color logos, and hi-vis pieces where embroidery would compromise reflectivity.
