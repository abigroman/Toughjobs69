Pricing & Costs

How much does custom software development cost?

The software licenses run separately from Toughjobs' work: entry platforms (Jobber, Housecall Pro) run $50–$200/user/month, mid-market (ServiceTitan, FieldEdge, Service Fusion) runs $200–$400/user/month, and enterprise/custom runs $400+/user/month. Toughjobs setup and configuration is $3,500–$12,000 one-time, and ongoing optimization and training runs $500–$1,500/month.

What factors determine the price of a software project?

Which platform tier fits your fleet size (1–5 trucks vs. 5–30 vs. multi-location), how much workflow customization and price-book building is needed, and how much team training and marketing integration is in scope.

Do you offer fixed-price, milestone-based, or time-and-materials billing?

Setup and configuration is priced as a one-time project ($3,500–$12,000); ongoing optimization and training runs as a monthly retainer ($500–$1,500/mo) on top of it.

Are hosting, third-party services, licenses, maintenance, and support additional costs?

Yes — the software platform's own subscription (per user, per month) is billed directly by that platform, separate from Toughjobs' setup and ongoing optimization fees.

Timeline & Completion

How long does implementation take?

A clean setup for a small shop takes 3–4 weeks. Mid-market platforms with full price book, memberships, and integrations run 6–10 weeks. Enterprise builds can be 3–6 months. Rushing this is how you end up abandoning the software six months later.

Our Process

What's the biggest mistake contractors make?

Buying the biggest platform they can afford and assuming it'll fix operational problems on its own. Software amplifies whatever process you already have. If your process is a mess, expensive software just makes an expensive mess. Fix the workflow first, then automate it.

What does your software development process involve?

Platform selection matched to your trade, size, and revenue; a workflow build covering job types, capacity rules, and drive-time buffers; price book configuration; team training; and marketing integration so leads and jobs flow through one system.

How do you test the software before launch?

Configuration is validated against your actual dispatch, invoicing, and reporting needs before rollout — the dispatch board specifically is tuned with job types and capacity rules so the team trusts it instead of keeping a paper backup.

What happens during deployment and launch?

Team training happens as part of the rollout, since adoption — not the software itself — is 70% of whether the system actually works.

Requirements & Preparation

Who from my business should participate in planning, testing, and approvals?

Whoever runs dispatch day to day, since the configuration is built around how your specific techs and jobs actually flow — not a generic template.

How should existing data be prepared for migration?

Customers, equipment history, open jobs, and accounting records are mapped and migrated as part of a platform switch, with some manual cleanup typically needed on messy or inconsistent records.

FieldEdge is a solid middle ground. We match the platform to your team size, revenue, and how much reporting you actually need — not to whoever's paying the biggest referral fee.

Will the software be customized for my workflows and users?

Yes — job types, capacity rules, drive-time buffers, price book, and reporting dashboards are all configured around your actual operation, not left at platform defaults.

Can you build web, mobile, desktop, cloud, or internal applications?

The core service is configuring established field service platforms (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, FieldEdge, Service Fusion) rather than building custom software from scratch.
