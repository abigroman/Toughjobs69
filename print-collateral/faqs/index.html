Pricing & Costs

How much does print design and production cost?

Design starts at $249 for a single piece or $899 for a full brand kit. By piece: business cards (design + 500 prints) run $250–$500, door hangers (design + 1,000 prints) run $450–$800, yard signs (design + 25 signs with stakes) run $400–$700, an EDDM postcard campaign (5,000 pieces) runs $1,800–$2,800, and a full print brand package (cards, hangers, signs, postcards, brochure, folder) runs $3,500–$6,500.

What factors determine the price of a print project?

Piece type, quantity, and finish — a single-piece design is priced differently from a full brand kit, and printing/mailing is quoted per run based on quantity and stock.

Are design and printing priced separately?

They're quoted together per project — design is a flat starting price, and printing/mailing is quoted per run based on quantity and stock, with real numbers given up front and no markup hidden behind the scenes.

Are proofs, revisions, shipping, and delivery included?

You approve a proof before anything goes to print or mail — we handle design, print, finishing, and mailing straight to the neighborhoods you want to win, including building the mailing list and targeting.

Our Process

How do I make print not look cheap?

Three things. Heavy stock (16pt minimum for cards, 14pt for hangers), full-bleed professional design instead of a Word document, and consistent branding across every piece. Cheap print screams cheap contractor, and that impression sticks.

What's the best neighborhood strategy after a big job?

We call it the "circle strategy." Yard sign in the customer's yard for two weeks. Door hangers on 20 neighbors the day the job wraps. EDDM postcard hits the surrounding two carrier routes 10 days later. Every touchpoint references the completed work. That's how one job becomes five.

What's the biggest print mistake contractors make?

Cluttered design. Trying to list every service, every certification, every discount on one card. The best-performing pieces have one message, one call to action, one phone number, and a lot of white space. Say less, sell more.

How do you select the appropriate format, paper, finish, and quantity?

By what the piece needs to do — door hangers use heavy die-cut card stock for a 15–25 house neighborhood drop, yard signs use weatherproof double-sided corrugated plastic with H-stakes, and EDDM postcards are priced and routed through USPS for carrier-route saturation.

Will I approve a proof before printing begins?

Yes, always — you approve a proof; we handle print, finishing, and delivery from there.

Results & Expectations

Does direct mail still work in 2026?

Yes, and better than it did five years ago because so few businesses use it anymore. Response rates on well-targeted trade postcards typically run 0.5–2%, which crushes most digital channels on a per-lead-cost basis. The key is targeting — blanket mailing entire zip codes is wasteful. Saturating carrier routes around completed jobs is not.

How do I track if print is actually working?

Use a dedicated tracking phone number on each print campaign (not your main line). QR codes with UTM parameters if you're driving to a landing page. Ask every incoming caller "how'd you hear about us?" and log it in the CRM. Within 90 days you'll know exactly what's producing.

How do you ensure images and text will print clearly?

Design is treated as one project with the distribution plan, not handed off to a cheap online printer that just prints whatever you upload without catching amateur-looking issues before they go to press.
