Pricing & Costs

How much do SEO services cost?

Pricing runs in tiers: Local Starter $800–$1,500/mo (GBP optimization, 5–8 core service/city pages, basic technical fixes, monthly reporting), Standard Campaign $1,800–$3,500/mo, Aggressive Growth $3,500–$6,500/mo, and Multi-Location/Franchise $6,500–$15,000+/mo. A one-time SEO audit runs $1,500–$3,500.

What factors determine the cost of an SEO campaign?

Scope drives the price — how many service and city pages you need, how much content you want published per month, how aggressive the link-building needs to be, and whether you're a single-location shop or multi-location/franchise.

Is SEO billed monthly, by project, or both?

Both. Ongoing campaigns are billed monthly by tier. A one-time SEO audit ($1,500–$3,500) is available as a standalone project if you want a technical, content, and competitive audit with a 90-day action plan before committing to a retainer.

Are content creation, technical fixes, and link acquisition included in the price?

Yes, bundled by tier — every tier includes technical fixes; Standard and above add ongoing blog content and link acquisition, scaling up with each tier.

Timeline & Completion

How long until SEO actually produces leads?

For local trade searches, expect early movement in 60–90 days, meaningful lead volume by month 4–6, and full campaign maturity around month 9–12. Anyone promising page one in 30 days is selling paid ads, black-hat tactics, or a fantasy.

How soon can you start on my SEO?

Most SEO engagements kick off within 1–2 weeks of signing. Exact start date depends on current workload — we will give you a real date on the strategy call, not a placeholder.*

How long does the initial SEO setup and technical audit take?

The technical audit, on-page fixes, and initial service-page work typically take 2–4 weeks with AI-assisted workflows. That is the work we control the speed of — where you actually rank afterward runs on Google's own timeline, not ours.*

How long should a business continue investing in SEO?

SEO is a long-term asset, not a project with a finish line — you own the pages, content, and rankings you build, and they keep compounding as long as the work continues. Most contractors run it alongside paid ads rather than treating it as a short campaign.

* Estimated. Current workload and holiday scheduling can shift these dates — we'll confirm exact timing on your call.

What's Included

What is included with your SEO service?

Four layers: technical SEO (crawlability, speed, Core Web Vitals, schema), on-page SEO (a page for every service and every city you serve), content (an editorial calendar built around the questions customers Google before they call), and links and authority (local news, supplier pages, chamber and BBB listings, trade associations).

Does your service include keyword research?

Yes — every service page is written for the exact phrase a customer types, not a generic industry term.

Do you handle technical SEO?

Yes. Before any content goes up, we make sure Google can crawl the site, render it fast, and understand it — fixing broken pages, cleaning up redirects, tightening Core Web Vitals, and implementing LocalBusiness and Service schema.

Do you create new service pages, location pages, or articles?

Yes. Every service you offer gets its own page written for the exact search, every city or neighborhood you serve gets its own page with real local context, and blog content is built around a 12-month editorial calendar tied to seasonal demand.

Do you help earn or build authoritative links?

Yes — local news mentions, supplier and manufacturer partner pages, chamber and BBB listings, trade association profiles, and earned community-sponsorship coverage. No spammy directories, no PBNs, nothing that risks a penalty.

Our Process

Do I need to write blog posts every week?

No. Trade SEO wins on service and city pages plus a steady drip of high-intent content — 2 to 4 pieces a month targeting real customer questions. Publishing daily fluff is a waste of your budget.

What does your SEO process involve?

Technical SEO first (so Google can crawl and understand the site), then on-page SEO (a page per service and city), then content (an editorial calendar tied to seasonal demand), then links and authority (earned local coverage, not spammy directories).

How do you choose the keywords to target?

Around real search intent — the specific phrases homeowners and property managers actually type when they need your trade in your service area, not generic industry terms.

How do you decide which pages to improve or create?

Every service you offer gets its own page, and every city or neighborhood you serve gets its own page with real local context — not the same template with the city name swapped out.

How often will work be performed on my website?

It scales by tier — Standard Campaign includes 2 blog posts a month, Aggressive Growth includes 4+ pieces of content a month plus heavier link building and quarterly strategy resets.

Customization & Options

Is SEO better than Google Ads?

They're different tools. Ads produce leads today at a per-lead cost. SEO produces leads for years at a declining cost per lead. Most contractors we work with run both — ads for immediate cash flow, SEO for long-term margin. Cutting SEO because ads work is like paying rent forever instead of buying the shop.

Will my SEO strategy be customized for my business?

Yes. Campaigns are built around your capacity, your margin services, and the searches that actually put both to work — not a one-size-fits-all template.

Can SEO focus on particular services, products, locations, or customer groups?

Yes — that's the core of on-page work: a dedicated page for each service and each city or neighborhood you serve.

Can you coordinate SEO with website design, paid ads, or content marketing?

Yes. Most contractors we work with run SEO and paid ads together — ads for immediate cash flow, SEO for long-term margin — with the channels kept distinct in reporting.

Results & Expectations

What if my competitors have been doing SEO for years?

You can still win. Most established local competitors got to page one and stopped working. We audit their gaps — thin service pages, weak local content, aging backlink profiles — and build a plan to outflank them on the specific searches that drive the most valuable jobs.

What SEO results should I realistically expect?

Milestone-based expectations, not a 30-day promise — think 6-month and 12-month checkpoints. Anyone selling first-page rankings in 30 days is selling paid ads, black-hat tactics, or a fantasy.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No agency controls a ranking position. We can improve the signals a business controls, document the work, and track visibility and inquiries — without presenting a guaranteed placement as fact.

Ownership & Access

Do I own the SEO work if I leave?

With us, yes. You own your site, your content, your Google Business Profile, and your analytics accounts. Some agencies build content on their own domains or use rented assets — always ask this before you sign, because it decides who owns your leads five years from now.

Policies & Guarantees

Do you use methods that follow search engine guidelines?

Yes — white-hat link building only, and every link is explainable before it goes live.

Do you purchase links or use automated link schemes?

No. No spammy directories, no PBNs (private blog networks), nothing that risks a Google penalty.

What is your cancellation policy for ongoing SEO services?

Month-to-month or short-term contracts after an initial ramp period — SEO takes time to build, so the first stretch is structured around getting the foundation in place, not locking you into a long contract before you've seen results.

Performance & Reporting

How do you measure SEO performance?

Three layers: a baseline (starting assets, access, tracking, market, budget, and constraints before work begins), a work log (what changed, when, who approved it, and what still depends on you or another vendor), and verified outcomes (calls and forms connected to lead quality or booked work when the data is available).

How often will I receive an SEO report?

Monthly reporting is standard across every tier.

Which rankings, traffic sources, conversions, and leads will the report include?

Reports focus on leads and booked revenue, not just rankings and traffic — vanity ranking screenshots for keywords no one searches don't make the cut.

Can you track phone calls, form submissions, and other conversions from organic search?

Yes — call tracking analysis is part of the Standard Campaign tier and above, connecting calls and forms back to the pages and searches that produced them.
