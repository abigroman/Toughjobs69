Straight answers

Flyers & Rack Cards FAQ

What should we provide for a flyers and rack cards quote?

Provide the intended use, approximate quantity, deadline, delivery location, available artwork, and any known production specifications. Important planning details include display environment, reading distance, service priority, offer, and restocking plan.

Does Toughjobs handle both design and production for flyers and rack cards?

The scope can include message planning, copy, design, proof preparation, and production coordination. The quote should state which services, materials, shipping, mailing, installation, or vendor costs are included.

What should be checked before the piece goes to production?

Review spelling, phone numbers, URLs, QR destinations, offer terms, and brand details. The production check should also confirm size, orientation, copy hierarchy, stock, finish, quantity, and display holder.
