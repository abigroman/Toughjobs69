Timeline & Completion

How long does it take to set up and optimize my Google Business Profile?

The setup and optimization work itself — categories, service list, service area, photos, initial citations — typically wraps in 1–2 weeks with AI-assisted workflows. That is the work we control the speed of; where you actually rank afterward runs on Google’s own timeline, not ours.*

How soon can you start working on my local SEO?

Most local SEO engagements kick off within 1–2 weeks of signing. Exact start date depends on current workload and where you land in the queue — we will give you a real date on the strategy call, not a placeholder.*

How long does local SEO take to produce results?

Local ranking movement depends on your starting position, competition, and market — GBP fixes and citation cleanup show up fastest, while city-page authority and review volume build over months.

How long does it take to improve visibility in Google Maps?

It varies by starting condition — a GBP with major missing fields or inconsistent citations can see faster early movement once fixed, while breaking into a competitive map pack against established competitors takes longer and compounds over time.

Map pack results are hyper-local — proximity to the searcher, not just overall ranking, affects which three businesses show. That's exactly why a real provider tracks rankings across a geo-grid instead of from a single office IP.

Getting Started

How is local SEO different from regular SEO?

Regular SEO focuses on organic blue-link rankings — often nationally. Local SEO focuses on the map pack and geo-modified searches ("plumber Bettendorf"). They overlap, but the tactics, tools, and ranking factors are different. Most contractors need both, weighted heavily toward local.

Requirements & Preparation

What if I don't have a storefront customers visit?

You use a service-area business (SAB) profile instead of a storefront listing. You hide your address, list your service radius, and optimize the same way. Google explicitly supports contractors who operate this way — it just requires a different setup.

What access do you need to manage my local SEO?

Access to your Google Business Profile and website; existing citation logins are helpful but not required since we audit and correct citations directly.

What business documents might be needed for profile verification or reinstatement?

Standard Google verification documentation (varies by case) — this is exactly the kind of situation where managing a GBP through a suspension appeal requires specific experience.

What information do you need about my locations, service areas, hours, and services?

Your full service list, the exact towns/cities you serve, real business hours, and any local landmarks or details that make each service-area page genuinely specific to that location.

Results & Expectations

What is the "map pack" and why does everyone talk about it?

The map pack (also called the "local 3-pack") is the block of three local business listings Google shows on a map for local-intent searches. For trades, it typically captures 40–60% of all clicks on the search results page. If you're not in it, you're fighting for the remainder.

Do reviews really move rankings that much?

Yes. Review count, star rating, review recency, and review keywords are all confirmed local ranking factors. Beyond that, a listing with 187 reviews at 4.8 stars gets clicked 3–5x more than one with 12 reviews at 4.4 — so even if rankings tied, the review-strong business wins the customer.

Can you guarantee a top-three Google Maps ranking?

No agency controls a map position. Local results change with the searcher, location, relevance, competition, profile quality, reputation, and Google updates. We can improve the signals a business controls, document the work, and track visibility and inquiries without presenting a guaranteed placement as fact.

Why does my business appear in Maps for some searches but not others?

Map pack results are hyper-local and depend on the searcher's exact location relative to yours — that's why real tracking uses a geo-grid across your whole service area instead of one single-point check.

Can local SEO increase calls, direction requests, and website visits?

Yes — reporting is built around rankings plus calls, not vanity metrics, so the connection between visibility and actual inquiries is tracked directly.

What can cause a Google Business Profile to lose visibility or become suspended?

Inconsistent NAP data across directories, incomplete profile fields, or policy violations can all cost visibility or trigger a suspension — managing a GBP through the suspension appeal process is a specific skill we handle.
