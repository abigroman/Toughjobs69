The rundown

LOCAL SEO FOR CONTRACTORS

Local SEO is the difference between showing up when someone searches "electrician near me" — or being invisible, even if you're three towns away doing great work. For a contractor, it's one of the best ways to spend a marketing dollar. Most of your competitors aren't doing it right, which makes it easier for you to win.

WHY THE GOOGLE MAP MATTERS SO MUCH

When someone searches "furnace repair Davenport," Google shows three things first: paid ads, a map with three local businesses on it (called the "map pack"), and regular search results below that. Most people search on their phone, and on a phone that map can fill the whole screen. If your business isn't one of those three spots, most people never scroll down far enough to find you.

The people looking at that map are ready to hire, fast. Someone with a leaking pipe at 6 p.m. isn't shopping around — they're calling the first two businesses they see and hiring whoever answers the phone and can show up soon. Local SEO is what gets your business into those first two taps, and keeps it there.

WHAT TO LOOK FOR IN A LOCAL SEO PROVIDER

Local SEO has more scams in it than almost any other kind of marketing. It's easy for someone to show you a screenshot of a good ranking, and hard for you to tell if it's real or faked. A trustworthy provider checks your ranking from many spots around your service area — not just from their own office.

  • Checks your ranking from many spots around town, not just one
  • Knows how to get a suspended Google listing back online
  • Tracks real phone calls from your Google listing, not just "views"
  • Writes real content for each town, not the same page with the city name swapped
  • Can show you proof of how many reviews you're getting each month
  • Stays current on Google's rules, which change often
