Straight answers

Stickers & Decals FAQ

What should we provide for a stickers and decals quote?

Provide the intended use, approximate quantity, deadline, delivery location, available artwork, and any known production specifications. Important planning details include surface, indoor or outdoor use, expected wear, removal needs, size, shape, and adhesive.

Does Toughjobs handle both design and production for stickers and decals?

The scope can include message planning, copy, design, proof preparation, and production coordination. The quote should state which services, materials, shipping, mailing, installation, or vendor costs are included.

What should be checked before the piece goes to production?

Review spelling, phone numbers, URLs, QR destinations, offer terms, and brand details. The production check should also confirm material, laminate, cut line, white ink, artwork scale, quantity, and application surface.
