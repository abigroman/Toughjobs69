Pricing & Costs

How much does a professionally designed website cost?

Pricing runs in tiers based on scope. Template Refresh (refreshing an existing site) runs $500–$900. A Standard AI-Assisted Build (up to 10–15 pages) runs $1,000–$2,000. A Multi-Service Build (up to 25–40 pages) runs $2,000–$4,000. A Multi-Location Build (up to 40–50 pages, multiple location sections) runs $3,500–$6,000. Custom functionality — scheduling, payments, customer portals, calculators, or software integrations — is quoted separately. Non-profit organizations get 50% off every tier.

What factors determine the cost of a website?

Cost depends on the number of real services, the content and photography already available, required integrations, and the condition of your current site. We define the page list, deliverables, ownership, and fee before work starts so the proposal can be compared without hidden platform charges.

What is included in the quoted price?

Every quote covers discovery around your services, service area, and lead handling; mobile-first page planning with a clear call and estimate path; service-page copy, on-page SEO, analytics, and form or call tracking setup; and launch support with a full handoff of the site, domain, and access you own.

Do you offer a discount for non-profit organizations?

Yes — non-profit organizations get 50% off every pricing tier, from a Template Refresh through a Multi-Location Build.

Not always. Results & Expectations

Will the site rank in Google once it launches?

The site will be built to rank — clean code, proper schema, service and city pages, fast load times. Ranking itself is a function of ongoing SEO, backlinks, reviews, and content. A great site is the foundation; SEO is the fuel. Most contractors see meaningful movement within 90–180 days of launch when both are in place.

Will my website appear in Google search results?

The site is built to rank — clean code, proper schema, service and city pages, fast load times. Ranking itself is a function of ongoing SEO, backlinks, reviews, and content on top of that foundation; most contractors see meaningful movement within 90–180 days of launch when both are in place.

Can you guarantee that my website will rank on the first page of Google?

No, and any agency that promises that is selling you a fantasy. A great site is the foundation; ranking depends on ongoing SEO work, backlinks, reviews, and content layered on top of it.

Will a new website help my business generate more leads or sales?

That's the design goal — more booked calls from a phone number in 6+ spots and forms that actually convert mobile traffic, and a lower cost per lead since a fast, optimized site needs less ad spend to get the same calls.

Will the website load quickly?

Sub-2-second load time on cellular is the target on every build — compressed images, deferred scripts, no bloated page builders, tested on throttled connections rather than a studio fiber line.

How will I know whether my website is performing successfully?

Analytics and form or call tracking are wired into the build so you can see which pages, ads, and keywords are actually producing calls and quote requests — not just traffic.
