Pricing & Costs

How much does remarketing campaign management cost?

A Single Reactivation Campaign runs $1,500–$4,000 flat (one campaign, one segment). A Seasonal Campaign Program runs $1,200–$2,500/mo (four campaigns a year, ongoing list hygiene). A Full Reactivation Retainer runs $2,500–$5,000/mo (monthly campaigns across multiple segments, dedicated strategist). Performance-Based Reactivation takes 10–20% of booked revenue with little or no upfront fee. A one-time List Audit & Strategy runs $750–$2,000 flat.

Is advertising spend separate from the management fee?

There's no ad spend involved — reactivation runs through email and SMS to your own existing customer list, not paid advertising platforms.

What minimum audience size and advertising budget are recommended?

500 contacts is a workable floor; 2,000+ is where campaigns start reliably producing five-figure returns. Under 500, focus on lead generation and building the list first.

Are campaign setup, creative, landing pages, and tracking included?

Yes for the Single Campaign tier and above — list cleanup, copy, send, and reporting are bundled together, not billed as separate line items.

Toughjobs runs reactivation campaigns for trade contractors across the Midwest.

Timeline & Completion

How long does it take to set up a remarketing campaign?

List cleanup, segmentation, and campaign copy typically take 1–2 weeks with AI-assisted workflows, depending on how messy the starting list is.*

How long does it take to build an audience large enough for remarketing?

500 contacts is a workable floor to start — if your list is smaller than that, the better first move is building the list before running a reactivation campaign.

How soon can campaigns begin producing useful performance data?

Bookings typically start within 24–72 hours of the first send, with the bulk of booked revenue landing in the first 14 days and a long tail over the following 30–60 days.

How soon can you start?

Most reactivation campaigns kick off within 1–2 weeks of signing. Exact start date depends on current workload — we will give you a real date on the strategy call, not a placeholder.*

* Estimated. Current workload and holiday scheduling can shift these dates — we'll confirm exact timing on your call.

Getting Started

How do I get started with remarketing?

A one-time List Audit & Strategy ($750–$2,000) is a good first step if you want to see what's in your database before committing to a full campaign.

What is the difference between remarketing and retargeting?

On this page, remarketing/reactivation means emailing and texting your own existing customer list (past clients, quoted leads, dormant contacts) — not online ad retargeting to anonymous website visitors.

What's Included

What is included with your remarketing service?

List cleanup and compliance (deduping, scrubbing bad numbers, honoring opt-outs), segmentation (by service type, last job date, ticket size, warranty cycle), campaign design (win-back, seasonal, referral), and multi-touch SMS/email orchestration with full tracking.

Will you install and test audience and conversion tracking?

Yes — opens, clicks, replies, and booked jobs are all tracked so you know exactly what a campaign earned.

Do you create the ads, copy, images, and video?

Campaign copy is written for the trade, the season, and the specific segment — generic blasts get ignored and burn the list, so nothing goes out untailored.

Who It's For

How big does my list need to be for reactivation to make sense?

500 contacts is a workable floor. 2,000+ is where campaigns start reliably producing five-figure returns. Under 500 and you're better off focusing on lead generation and building the list first.

What types of businesses benefit from remarketing?

Trade contractors with an existing database of past customers, quoted-but-never-booked leads, or warranty-cycle customers sitting unused in their CRM.

Is remarketing useful for a local service business?

Yes — it's often the cheapest revenue a contractor has, since past customers already know and trust you and cost pennies to reach by text or email versus $75–$400 for a new lead from ads.

Our Process

How do you decide which visitors or customers to target?

Segmentation by service type, last job date, ticket size, quoted-but-never-booked status, and warranty-cycle timing — not one blast to the whole list.

How do you tailor messages to different stages of the customer journey?

Win-back sequences target customers you haven't seen in 12–36 months, seasonal campaigns hit the right list at the right time (AC tune-ups in April, gutter cleaning in October), and referral activation asks happy past customers for names.

How often are audiences, creative, and bids optimized?

Multi-touch sequences run over 5–14 days (initial offer, reminder, last-chance, rest period), with everything tracked so campaigns can be iterated on based on what actually worked.

Requirements & Preparation

What if my database is a mess — spreadsheets, old CRM exports, handwritten notes?

That's normal. Part of the engagement is consolidating, cleaning, and segmenting whatever you have. A messy 3,000-contact database is still a goldmine once it's organized.

What access do you need to begin remarketing?

Access to your customer database or CRM export — whatever format it's in, including spreadsheets, old CRM exports, or handwritten notes.

What website traffic, customer data, or conversion history is needed?

Your existing customer list is the core input — a messy 3,000-contact database is still a goldmine once it's cleaned and segmented.

Do I need privacy and cookie-consent notices before tracking visitors?

Reactivation runs through email/SMS to your own list, so the compliance focus is TCPA and CAN-SPAM consent for messaging — opt-outs are honored and unengaged contacts are flagged before anything sends.

Customization & Options

Is SMS or email better for reactivation?

SMS wins for open rates and speed to booking, email wins for cost and detail. The best programs use both — SMS for time-sensitive offers and quick check-ins, email for longer content, seasonal reminders, and anyone who hasn't opted in to texts.

Can you create different campaigns for leads, customers, cart abandoners, and past purchasers?

Yes — win-back for dormant customers, seasonal for timing-specific offers, and referral activation for happy past customers, each written for the specific segment rather than one generic blast.

Can remarketing be coordinated with paid search, social media, email, and CRM follow-up?

Yes — campaigns run through your existing CRM data and can layer alongside other channels rather than operating as a standalone effort.

Results & Expectations

How fast do we see revenue from a reactivation campaign?

Bookings start coming in within 24–72 hours of the first send. Most campaigns generate the bulk of their booked revenue in the first 14 days, with a long tail of jobs booking over the following 30–60 days.

What results should I realistically expect from remarketing?

A well-run campaign can generate $20K–$100K in booked work within 30 days, at a fraction of new-lead advertising cost — because you've already paid to acquire these customers once.

Can you guarantee a particular conversion rate or return on advertising spend?

No guaranteed rate, but Performance-Based Reactivation (10–20% of booked revenue, little or no upfront fee) is available for large lists if you want the cost tied directly to results.

How do you determine whether remarketing contributed to a conversion?

Opens, clicks, replies, and booked jobs are tracked per campaign, so revenue can be directly attributed to the specific send rather than guessed at.

Ownership & Access

Who owns the advertising accounts, audiences, pixels, and campaign data?

You own your customer list and CRM data — reactivation campaigns work with your existing database, not a separate ad platform account.

What happens to the campaigns and audiences if I end the service?

Your customer list and any cleanup/segmentation work stays with you — it's your database, not something tied to continuing the service.

Security & Data Privacy

How is customer-list data protected?

List cleanup includes honoring opt-outs and flagging unengaged contacts, with TCPA and CAN-SPAM compliance treated as non-negotiable — a bad campaign to a non-consenting list can cost more in fines than the reactivation earns.

How long are remarketing audiences retained?

Segmented lists are maintained as an ongoing asset for repeat seasonal and win-back campaigns rather than discarded after a single send.

Policies & Guarantees

Will reactivation campaigns annoy my past customers?

Not if they're written like a helpful check-in instead of a sales blast. "Hey, it's been 18 months since we serviced your AC — want us to swing by for a tune-up before summer?" reads as service, not spam. Frequency and tone are what separate a welcomed message from an unsubscribe.

Will campaigns comply with advertising-platform and privacy requirements?

Yes — TCPA and CAN-SPAM compliance is treated as non-negotiable, with a documented process for list cleanup, opt-out honoring, and consent, not just "we'll be careful."

What happens if tracking, an audience, or an advertising account is restricted?

A sloppy sender reputation can push future emails to spam, which is exactly why list hygiene and compliance are treated as core parts of the service, not an afterthought.
