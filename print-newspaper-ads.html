The rundown

Newspaper and Publication Ads built around a specific job.

print ads for contractors should not begin with a generic layout. Newspaper and Publication Ads should be scoped from the moment of use backward. If the piece is for a local publication, association guide, program, or special section, the message, physical format, proof, and response path all need to support that one job. Copy written without that context usually becomes crowded or too vague to act on.

Before design, Toughjobs documents reader, placement, deadline, ad dimensions, service focus, and response path. The first proof is checked as a working communication tool: Can the intended reader understand it in the available time? Is the main action obvious? Does every claim match what the business can actually deliver?

Print Ads FAQ

What should we provide for a newspaper and publication ads quote?

Provide the intended use, approximate quantity, deadline, delivery location, available artwork, and any known production specifications. Important planning details include reader, placement, deadline, ad dimensions, service focus, and response path.

Does Toughjobs handle both design and production for newspaper and publication ads?

The scope can include message planning, copy, design, proof preparation, and production coordination. The quote should state which services, materials, shipping, mailing, installation, or vendor costs are included.

What should be checked before the piece goes to production?

Review spelling, phone numbers, URLs, QR destinations, offer terms, and brand details. The production check should also confirm publication specification, bleed, color mode, copy deadline, offer terms, and final file.
