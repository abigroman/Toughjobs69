The rundown

Brochures built around a specific job.

brochures for contractors should not begin with a generic layout. Brochures should be scoped from the moment of use backward. If the piece is for an estimate follow-up, sales appointment, referral packet, or service overview, the message, physical format, proof, and response path all need to support that one job. Copy written without that context usually becomes crowded or too vague to act on.

Before design, Toughjobs documents fold, page order, services included, proof required, and whether the piece will be mailed or handed over. The first proof is checked as a working communication tool: Can the intended reader understand it in the available time? Is the main action obvious? Does every claim match what the business can actually deliver?

Brochures FAQ

What should we provide for a brochures quote?

Provide the intended use, approximate quantity, deadline, delivery location, available artwork, and any known production specifications. Important planning details include fold, page order, services included, proof required, and whether the piece will be mailed or handed over.

Does Toughjobs handle both design and production for brochures?

The scope can include message planning, copy, design, proof preparation, and production coordination. The quote should state which services, materials, shipping, mailing, installation, or vendor costs are included.

What should be checked before the piece goes to production?

Review spelling, phone numbers, URLs, QR destinations, offer terms, and brand details. The production check should also confirm panel order, contact details, offer terms, folds, paper, finish, and mailing requirements.
