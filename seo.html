The rundown

SEO FOR CONTRACTORS AND TRADE BUSINESSES

Contractor SEO isn't about ranking for a national keyword. It's about owning the ten to forty searches homeowners and property managers actually type when they need your trade in your service area — and being the first name they see, call, and hire.

WHY CONTRACTOR SEO IS ITS OWN DISCIPLINE

SEO for a SaaS company is a long game of thought leadership and backlinks. SEO for a contractor is a knife fight over ten square miles. The intent is different — nobody is casually researching "emergency drain cleaning" on a Sunday morning. They have a problem, they have a phone, and they have thirty seconds of patience. Your job in that thirty seconds is to be the first result, look like a real business, and get the call.

Seasonality makes it harder. HVAC leads spike in July and January. Roofers get slammed after every storm. Plumbers ride steady with emergency peaks. A contractor SEO strategy that doesn't understand your booking calendar is going to spend the wrong money at the wrong time. Real campaigns are built around your capacity, your best-paying jobs, and the searches that put both to work.

What it takes

WHAT A REAL SEO CAMPAIGN COVERS

Fixing your website so Google trusts it

Before we write a word, we make sure your site loads fast and Google can actually read it. That means fixing broken pages, bad redirects, and slow load times. If the plumbing's wrong, no amount of content is going to drain the sink.

The pages that rank

Every service you offer gets its own page, written for the exact phrase a customer types. Every city or neighborhood you serve gets its own page, with real local details — not the same template with the city name swapped out. Titles, headers, and links are all built the way Google reads a trade site.

Content — the reason to rank

Blog content for contractors isn't fluff. It's "How much does a new AC unit cost in Peoria?" and "What to do when your basement floods." These are the questions your customers Google before they call, and answering them well gets them thinking about you weeks before they ever type your name. We build a 12-month content calendar tied to your busy season.

Real links from real businesses

A link is when another website sends people to yours. Good links help Google see that your business is real and trusted.

We never buy links. Bought links can vanish when the payment stops, and Google may push your site down for using them.

How we get good links:

  • Ask your suppliers to list you as a trusted installer
  • Get your business listed by trade groups you belong to
  • Help local news sites with tips and expert advice
  • Ask websites that mention your company to link to you
  • Create helpful guides other websites want to share
  • Earn mentions from local groups you work with

No fake websites. No paid posts. No junk business lists. Real links take more work, but they give people a reason to visit your site — and give Google a reason to trust it.

WHAT TO LOOK FOR IN AN SEO PROVIDER

The SEO industry is full of monthly-report factories that produce beautiful PDFs and zero leads. Real SEO is measured in ringing phones, filled forms, and booked jobs — not in vanity ranking screenshots for keywords no one searches. If a provider won't talk about leads and revenue, they're the wrong provider.

  • Reports on leads and booked revenue, not just rankings and traffic
  • Shows you actual case studies from trade contractors with before/after numbers
  • Handles your whole website, content, and links under one roof — no finger-pointing
  • Sets 6-month and 12-month milestone expectations, not "results in 30 days" promises
  • Only builds real links — will show you every one before it goes live
  • Gives you month-to-month or short-term contracts after an initial ramp period
