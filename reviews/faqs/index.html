Yes — both Toughjobs tiers include the SMS/email system and QR assets, not a separate software subscription on top.

Results & Expectations

Can you remove a negative review?

A business can flag content that appears to violate a platform's policy, but honest criticism can't legitimately be deleted. The practical response is to acknowledge the concern, avoid private details, offer a direct resolution path, and fix any underlying process problem.

How can reviews influence customer trust and local search visibility?

A move from a 3.9 to a 4.6 star rating roughly doubles call volume, and a move from 40 to 200+ reviews often doubles it again. Google feeds the same signals — review velocity, keyword-rich text, owner responses — into map pack rankings.

What should I do if I believe a review is false, abusive, or from someone who was not a customer?

Flag it — content that violates a platform's policy (non-customer, profanity, competitor-posted) can be reported for removal. Reputation recovery service includes formal review dispute filings for exactly this.

Support & Maintenance

What do we do about a bad review?

Respond publicly within 24 hours — calm, professional, offer to make it right offline. Never argue in the response thread. If the review violates Google's policy (competitor, non-customer, profanity) we file for removal. And the best defense is volume — twenty new 5-stars in the following month push it off the front page.

Policies & Guarantees

Is buying reviews ever okay?

Never. Google's algorithms and manual review team are extremely good at spotting bought reviews, and the penalty is removal from the map pack — sometimes permanently. Every review we generate comes from a verified customer who actually paid you for work.

Can we offer a discount for a review?

No. Every major platform prohibits incentivized reviews, and getting caught will tank your rating and potentially your entire listing. The only ethical ask is "if you were happy with the work, would you take 30 seconds to leave us a review?" That's enough — because if the work was actually good, they'll do it.

Do you offer incentives for reviews or filter customers based on expected ratings?

No. The same neutral request goes to every eligible customer — no review gating, no diverting unhappy customers to a private form while only satisfied ones get asked publicly, and no incentives offered for a positive review.

Will you create, purchase, or arrange fake reviews?

No. A real provider generates real reviews from real customers, faster than you'd get on your own — agencies selling reviews from overseas farms risk getting your entire Google Business Profile suspended.
