Plan before production

Direct Mailers built around a specific job.

direct mail for contractors should not begin with a generic layout. A printed piece earns its place when the situation is clear. For a seasonal offer, route-density campaign, new-service launch, or neighborhood follow-up, start with the person who will see it, what that person already knows, and the next step that is reasonable in that setting. That keeps the page from becoming a list of every service the company has ever offered.

Before design, Toughjobs documents audience, mailing area, offer, deadline, landing page, call tracking, and postal format. The first proof is checked as a working communication tool: Can the intended reader understand it in the available time? Is the main action obvious? Does every claim match what the business can actually deliver?

Production begins only after the authorized contact approves the message and specifications. That approval covers list source, mail class, dimensions, address area, offer terms, tracking route, and drop date. If a vendor or installer changes a requirement, the file is revised and approved again so the finished piece does not depend on an assumption hidden in an email thread.

Direct Mailers FAQ

What should we provide for a direct mailers quote?

Provide the intended use, approximate quantity, deadline, delivery location, available artwork, and any known production specifications. Important planning details include audience, mailing area, offer, deadline, landing page, call tracking, and postal format.

Does Toughjobs handle both design and production for direct mailers?

The scope can include message planning, copy, design, proof preparation, and production coordination. The quote should state which services, materials, shipping, mailing, installation, or vendor costs are included.

What should be checked before the piece goes to production?

Review spelling, phone numbers, URLs, QR destinations, offer terms, and brand details. The production check should also confirm list source, mail class, dimensions, address area, offer terms, tracking route, and drop date.
