Pricing & Costs

How much does an AI automation cost?

Starter Automation is $500–$1,500 setup plus $150–$300/mo. Standard Automation Stack is $2,500–$5,000 setup plus $400–$700/mo. Full Ops Automation is $6,000–$12,000 setup plus $800–$1,500/mo. An AI Voice/Chat Agent is $3,500–$8,000 setup plus $500–$1,200/mo. Custom integrations to other systems run $1,000–$4,000 one-time per system.

What factors determine the setup and ongoing price?

How many workflows you need (missed-call text-back alone vs. full pipeline automation with AI dispatching), whether you want an AI voice/chat agent answering calls, and how many systems need custom integration.

Are third-party software and AI usage fees included?

The monthly fee covers the automation platform and workflows; integration with your existing CRM (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, HubSpot) is included at the Standard tier and above, with custom integrations to other systems priced separately.

How is future expansion or modification priced?

Additional workflows or a jump to a higher tier (e.g., adding an AI voice/chat agent) are scoped and priced as add-ons rather than requiring you to rebuild from scratch.

Getting Started

What's the difference between automation and AI?

Automation runs pre-built workflows on triggers (missed call → send text). AI adds judgment — reading a message, understanding intent, generating a personalized response, or booking an appointment in a real conversation. Most contractors need both, and the right stack blends them.

Do you begin with a workflow or automation audit?

Yes — a baseline is recorded first: starting assets, access, tracking, market, budget, and operating constraints, before any workflow is built.

Can you help identify which business processes should be automated first?

Yes — missed-call text-back is typically the highest-ROI starting point, since it alone recovers 15–30% of leads that would otherwise be lost and pays for itself in the first month.

Can you improve an automation that another provider created?

Yes — we can build on top of what you already have instead of replacing it, connecting automation layers to your existing CRM so your team doesn't have to learn a new system or double-enter data.

What's Included

Do you map the current process before designing the automation?

Yes — a baseline of your starting assets, access, tracking, and constraints is documented before any workflow is built.

Will you create monitoring, alerts, approvals, and fallback procedures?

Yes — the workflow always needs a fallback: stop, flag, route to a person, or ask for clarification. New leads get an alert straight to your phone in addition to the automated response.

Is ongoing maintenance and optimization included?

Yes, at every tier — monthly fees ($150–$1,500/mo depending on tier) cover ongoing operation, not just the one-time setup.

Will you measure the time or cost saved by the automation?

Yes — a work log documents what changed and when, and verified outcomes connect the automation to lead quality or booked work when the data is available.

Who It's For

Which repetitive tasks are good candidates for automation?

Missed-call text-back, new lead auto-response, appointment reminders and confirmations, review requests, quote follow-up sequences, and seasonal reminders (tune-ups, gutter cleaning, filter changes).

Which tasks should not be fully automated?

Anything requiring judgment, complex sales conversations, or handling of sensitive, legal, or safety-related situations — automation removes repetitive work so your office can focus on the calls and quotes that actually need a human.

Our Process

How do you document the workflow, decisions, exceptions, and desired outcome?

Starting with a baseline (assets, access, tracking, constraints), then a documented work log of what changed, when, who approved it, and what still depends on you or another vendor.

How do you choose the appropriate AI model and automation tools?

Matched to your existing field service software (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro) rather than a generic one-size-fits-all template — trade workflows like dispatch, on-my-way texts, and warranty reminders aren't interchangeable across providers.

How are human review and escalation incorporated?

Every automated workflow needs a fallback — stop, flag, route to a person, or ask for clarification — and AI voice/chat agents specifically hand off to a human when the conversation needs one.

Results & Expectations

Will customers know they're talking to a bot?

The best setups don't try to hide it, and they don't need to. A friendly automated text that says "we missed your call, can we help?" reads like good service, not a robot. AI voice agents introduce themselves as an assistant and hand off to a human for anything complex.

How fast do we see results from missed-call text-back?

Immediately. The day it's live, you start recovering calls you would have lost. Most contractors book at least one additional job in the first week — often more than enough to cover the entire monthly cost.

Does this replace our office staff?

No. It removes the repetitive work — reminders, confirmations, review chasing, first-touch responses — so your office can focus on the calls and quotes that actually need a human. Most contractors use automation to grow without adding headcount, not to cut staff.

What results should I realistically expect from AI automation?

Missed-call text-back typically recovers 15–30% of leads that would otherwise be lost. Automated appointment reminders and review-request sequences can double monthly review flow and cut no-shows by half.

How do you estimate potential time savings, cost savings, or revenue impact?

Contacting a lead within 5 minutes makes you roughly 20x more likely to qualify them versus waiting 30 minutes — that response-speed math is the basis for most of the ROI estimate, along with recovered missed-call leads and reduced no-shows.

Will employees still need to review or manage the automation?

Yes for anything requiring judgment — automation handles the repeatable follow-up and reminders so your team can spend their time on the calls, quotes, and jobs that actually need a human.
