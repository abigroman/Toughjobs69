Door Hangers

Work a whole street. Perfect for “we’re in your neighborhood” drops and seasonal specials.

Door hangers are the highest-ROI print piece available to a trades business, and the reason is simple: after finishing a job, a crew can hang branded hangers on 15 to 25 neighboring homes for the cost of paper and twenty minutes.

Response rates on a well-targeted hanger campaign routinely run 1 to 3 percent, meaning nearly every completed job creates one or two additional leads on the same street. Toughjobs designs hangers with a neighborhood-specific angle — "we just worked on your street" performs consistently better than a generic company pitch — on heavy card stock with a proper die-cut hanger, not a folded flyer that looks cheap in someone's hand.

We handle the design, the copy, and the print production, and we can have a fresh run ready to go out the same week a big job wraps. Pair the hanger with a matching yard sign at the original job and the whole street sees your work twice before the paper ever leaves their hand.

The rundown

Door Hangers built around a specific job.

door hangers for contractors should not begin with a generic layout. Door Hangers should be scoped from the moment of use backward. If the piece is for a nearby job, canvassing route, maintenance reminder, or neighborhood introduction, the message, physical format, proof, and response path all need to support that one job. Copy written without that context usually becomes crowded or too vague to act on.

Before design, Toughjobs documents route, weather exposure, offer, service relevance, response path, and distribution responsibility. The first proof is checked as a working communication tool: Can the intended reader understand it in the available time? Is the main action obvious? Does every claim match what the business can actually deliver?

Straight answers

Door Hangers FAQ

What should we provide for a door hangers quote?

Provide the intended use, approximate quantity, deadline, delivery location, available artwork, and any known production specifications. Important planning details include route, weather exposure, offer, service relevance, response path, and distribution responsibility.

Does Toughjobs handle both design and production for door hangers?

The scope can include message planning, copy, design, proof preparation, and production coordination. The quote should state which services, materials, shipping, mailing, installation, or vendor costs are included.

What should be checked before the piece goes to production?

Review spelling, phone numbers, URLs, QR destinations, offer terms, and brand details. The production check should also confirm die cut, stock, coating, quantity, QR or phone destination, and delivery plan.
