## Overall Impression

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Dead exit link inside the 17-service panel**
Why it matters: `href="javascript:void(0)"` on the panel's "Back to home" link does nothing when clicked. It's the one explicit exit affordance built into a section deliberately structured as a mini sub-page — a visitor who scrolled in expecting a way back out gets nothing (Nielsen #3, User Control and Freedom).
Fix: Point it at a real anchor/scroll-to-top target, or remove it — a fake link is worse than no link.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P0] 17 services dumped with zero progressive disclosure at the page's single biggest decision point**
Why it matters: A first-time visitor has no way to tell which 2–3 of 17 services apply to them. This fails the cognitive-load "≤4 visible choices per decision point" rule at the most consequential point on the page — what does ToughJobs actually sell.
Fix: Default-collapse to the 4 category headers with their existing counts (07/05/04/01) as accordion triggers, expand-on-click per category; or add a short "what trade are you?" filter ahead of the full list.
Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`

**[P1] Low-contrast red-on-ink text fails WCAG AA**
Why it matters: The detector found 3 instances of `#C8262A` text on `#0A0F1C` background at 3.4:1 contrast, below the 4.5:1 minimum for body text. This directly hits accessibility-dependent visitors and is an objective, measurable failure, not a taste call.
Fix: Darken the background behind these instances, or reserve `#C8262A` for large/bold text and icons only in these spots (large text has a lower 3:1 threshold), or swap to white for the affected body-sized text.
Suggested command: `/impeccable audit`

**[P1] Persistent floating-widget stack occludes content**
Why it matters: Four always-on overlays (spinning "Start Assessment" badge top-right, "Image Finder" tab mid-right, "Ask ToughJobs" chat bottom-right, accessibility icon bottom-left) compete with an already-long page throughout the entire scroll. The live browser detector confirmed this isn't just visual clutter — it flagged actual text occlusion where the chat/consent layer overlaps page copy.
Fix: Audit whether all four widgets earn permanent screen real estate; convert lower-value ones (Image Finder) to scroll-triggered or section-scoped affordances instead of page-global fixtures, and fix the z-index/positioning that lets the chat layer sit on top of text.
Suggested command: `/impeccable quieter`

**[P2] Design-token drift: 26 instances break the system's own rules**
Why it matters: DESIGN.md's "No-Radius Rule" is stated as absolute ("zero border-radius on all interactive elements... the only exception is pill-shaped badges"), yet the detector found 2 elements at 6px/8px radius. Alongside that, 8 off-palette color uses and 16 off-scale font-sizes are drifting from the documented token system — small individually, but cumulatively this is exactly the kind of inconsistency that erodes the "precise, unambiguous" blueprint identity the brand is built on.
Fix: Sweep the 2 radius violations first (they break an explicit hard rule), then reconcile the color/font-size list against DESIGN.md tokens in a single pass.
Suggested command: `/impeccable polish`
