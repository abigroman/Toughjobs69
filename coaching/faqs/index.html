Pricing & Costs

How much does business coaching cost?

Crew Accelerator group coaching for service technicians is $98 per technician for a full year. Growth Partner is $1,854 per year.

Is coaching priced per session, by package, or through a monthly program?

Crew Accelerator is $98 per technician per year, with ongoing group coaching during active enrollment. Growth Partner is $1,854 per year for a private coaching partnership.

What is included in the coaching fee?

Crew Accelerator includes weekly group role-play calls for technicians while enrolled. They practice customer conversations and jobsite scenarios, spot warning signs early, and rehearse how to de-escalate issues, explain options, and agree on next steps. Growth Partner adds weekly private strategy calls, priority access, and done-with-you support for owners.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Crew Accelerator enrollment covers a full year, with coaching available while the technician remains enrolled. Growth Partner is also priced per year. Confirm enrollment and renewal terms for the plan you choose.

Getting Started

I'm too busy to add coaching calls to my week — is this really the right time?

Both plans meet weekly. Crew Accelerator gives technicians a place to practice jobsite and customer conversations; Growth Partner gives owners a regular time to work through business decisions and implementation. Choose the plan that fits your role and schedule.

Do you offer an introductory consultation?

Reach out through the contact page — the reason you're too busy for coaching is exactly why you need it, and the first conversation is about finding the right starting point.

How do you determine whether coaching is a good fit?

Crew Accelerator is designed for service technicians who want ongoing group coaching and jobsite role-play while enrolled. Growth Partner is for owners who need private, business-specific coaching.

Can you help me identify the most important issue to address first?

Yes — sales, pricing, and margin work usually pays back the entire coaching investment inside a quarter, since most contractors underprice by 15–30% without realizing it, so that's often the first lever pulled.

Who It's For

What if I've already been in business 20 years?

Some of the best coaching results happen with veteran owners who never formalized their systems because everything's always been in their head. A coach helps you document, delegate, and eventually exit — whether "exit" means sale, succession, or just finally taking a real vacation.

Who is Toughjobs coaching designed for?

Crew Accelerator group coaching is for service technicians. Growth Partner is for trade business owners who want private coaching and support.

Do you coach startups, small-business owners, managers, or established companies?

Service technicians can join Crew Accelerator. Trade business owners can choose Growth Partner for business-specific coaching and support.

What is the difference between coaching, consulting, mentoring, and training?

Toughjobs coaching is structured, ongoing guidance from someone who has actually built, run, or scaled a contracting company — not a generic framework borrowed from an MBA program or another coach who's never run a trade business.

Our Process

How are goals, priorities, and success measures established?

Through a baseline before work begins (starting assets, access, tracking, market, budget, constraints), then a documented work log of what changed and what's next.

What happens during a typical coaching session?

Crew Accelerator uses group role-play of customer conversations and jobsite problems so technicians can practice preventing and handling issues. Growth Partner provides weekly private strategy calls and weekly group coaching access.

How are action items and progress reviewed?

A work log documents what changed, when, who approved it, and what still depends on you — and verified outcomes connect the work to real business results rather than a stock progress report.

Requirements & Preparation

Do I have to change my software or vendors to work with a coach?

No. A good coach works with what you have. If your systems genuinely need to change, they'll tell you why and let you decide — they won't sell you their own preferred platform as a condition of coaching.

What financial, operational, marketing, or performance information should I provide?

Share the information relevant to your goals, such as job costing for pricing work, hiring processes for team development, and financial or performance numbers for quarterly reviews.

What happens if I do not complete an agreed action item?

Crew Accelerator provides group practice and feedback. Growth Partner includes weekly private strategy calls and priority direct access to help keep agreed actions moving.

Customization & Options

Group coaching or private partnership — which should I choose?

Crew Accelerator is ongoing group coaching for service technicians at $98 per technician per year. Growth Partner offers private coaching for owners who need business-specific support.

Will the coaching program be customized for my goals and industry?

Yes — both plans are trades-focused. Crew Accelerator is group coaching for service technicians, while Growth Partner addresses an owner's specific business goals.

Can coaching focus on marketing, operations, leadership, growth, or accountability?

Yes — the three core areas (sales/pricing/margin, hiring/HR/team building, operations/SOPs/growth planning) cover all of those, with emphasis set by what you need most.

Can coaching be combined with Toughjobs implementation services?

Results & Expectations

How long until I see ROI?

Pricing and sales work usually pays back inside 60–90 days. Hiring and systems work is a longer arc — six to twelve months to see the full compounding effect. If a coach promises overnight results, they're selling something else.

How will we measure whether coaching is working?

A documented baseline, a work log of what changed, and verified outcomes connecting the coaching to real business results — the same evidence standard applied across every Toughjobs service.

What responsibilities do I have for implementing the recommendations?

Coaching provides the plan, structure, and accountability, but you run the business day to day — the action items are yours to execute between sessions.

Security & Data Privacy

Will information discussed during coaching remain confidential?

Business and financial details discussed in sessions are treated as confidential to the coaching relationship.

Policies & Guarantees

What happens if coaching is not a good fit?

The right plan depends on who needs coaching and what support they need. Crew Accelerator serves service technicians; Growth Partner serves owners. If a plan does not fit, raise that before enrolling.

Does coaching include legal, tax, accounting, investment, or mental-health advice?

No — coaching covers pricing, sales, hiring, operations, and growth planning for the business. For legal, tax, or accounting matters, a licensed professional is the right resource.
