Pricing & Costs

How much does business coaching cost?

Group Coaching runs $500–$1,200/mo. 1:1 Coaching (monthly) runs $1,500–$3,500/mo. 1:1 Coaching (executive) runs $4,000–$8,000/mo. An Intensive/Bootcamp (2–3 days) runs $3,500–$12,000 flat. An Annual Coaching Retainer runs $25,000–$75,000/yr.

Is coaching priced per session, by package, or through a monthly program?

Both, depending on format — ongoing programs (Group, 1:1 monthly, 1:1 executive, Annual Retainer) are priced monthly or annually, while the Intensive/Bootcamp is a flat one-time fee for a focused 2–3 day engagement.

What is included in the coaching fee?

It scales by tier: Group Coaching includes monthly calls, peer accountability, curriculum, and community access. 1:1 monthly adds twice-monthly private sessions, direct text/email access, a custom action plan, and a quarterly deep-dive. 1:1 executive adds weekly sessions, on-site visits, a KPI dashboard, and hiring/org design support.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Coaching is billed as one payment per plan rather than recurring or financed — check the specific tier's term when you sign up.

Timeline & Completion

How often will coaching sessions take place?

Monthly group calls for Group Coaching, twice-monthly private sessions for 1:1 monthly, and weekly sessions for 1:1 executive.

How soon can coaching begin?

Most new clients have their first session within 1–2 weeks of signing up. Exact start date depends on current workload — we will give you a real date when you reach out.*

Can I schedule a one-time strategy session?

The Intensive/Bootcamp format (2–3 days, $3,500–$12,000 flat) is the closest fit — focused deep-work on one area like a pricing rebuild, hiring system, or SOP buildout, with real deliverables at the end.

* Estimated. Current workload and holiday scheduling can shift these dates — we'll confirm exact timing on your call.

Getting Started

I'm too busy to add coaching calls to my week — is this really the right time?

The reason you're too busy is exactly why you need it. Coaching creates the two hours a month that build the systems that give you back ten hours a week. If you wait until you're "less busy," you'll be having this same conversation in three years.

Do you offer an introductory consultation?

Reach out through the contact page — the reason you're too busy for coaching is exactly why you need it, and the first conversation is about finding the right starting point.

How do you determine whether coaching is a good fit?

Group works well under $1M in revenue with structured curriculum and peer accountability. 1:1 makes more sense above $1M when your problems get specific to your company and need custom answers, not general frameworks.

Can you help me identify the most important issue to address first?

Yes — sales, pricing, and margin work usually pays back the entire coaching investment inside a quarter, since most contractors underprice by 15–30% without realizing it, so that's often the first lever pulled.

What's Included

What is included with your coaching service?

Three core areas: sales, pricing, and margin (real job costing, pricing models, sales training); hiring, HR, and team building (recruiting pipelines, compensation, onboarding SOPs); and operations, SOPs, and growth planning (written processes, KPI dashboards, quarterly/annual planning).

Will I receive a written plan, exercises, templates, or other resources?

Yes — a custom action plan is part of the 1:1 monthly tier and above, with a KPI dashboard added at the executive tier.

Is support available between scheduled sessions?

Yes, at the 1:1 monthly tier and above — direct text/email access to your coach between sessions.

Will you review business plans, marketing, systems, or performance data?

Yes — a financial review and KPI dashboard are part of the 1:1 executive tier, alongside hiring and org design support.

Who It's For

What if I've already been in business 20 years?

Some of the best coaching results happen with veteran owners who never formalized their systems because everything's always been in their head. A coach helps you document, delegate, and eventually exit — whether "exit" means sale, succession, or just finally taking a real vacation.

Who is Toughjobs coaching designed for?

Trade business owners — the whole program is built around trade-specific realities like job costing, seasonal cash flow, and crew hiring, not generic MBA-program frameworks.

Do you coach startups, small-business owners, managers, or established companies?

Both ends — Group Coaching fits owners under $1M in revenue, while 1:1 and executive tiers fit established companies with more specific, higher-stakes problems.

What is the difference between coaching, consulting, mentoring, and training?

Toughjobs coaching is structured, ongoing guidance from someone who has actually built, run, or scaled a contracting company — not a generic framework borrowed from an MBA program or another coach who's never run a trade business.

Our Process

How are goals, priorities, and success measures established?

Through a baseline before work begins (starting assets, access, tracking, market, budget, constraints), then a documented work log of what changed and what's next.

What happens during a typical coaching session?

It depends on the tier — group calls follow a curriculum on sales, pricing, and ops with peer accountability; 1:1 sessions work from your custom action plan; executive sessions include KPI review and financial review.

How are action items and progress reviewed?

A work log documents what changed, when, who approved it, and what still depends on you — and verified outcomes connect the work to real business results rather than a stock progress report.

Requirements & Preparation

Do I have to change my software or vendors to work with a coach?

No. A good coach works with what you have. If your systems genuinely need to change, they'll tell you why and let you decide — they won't sell you their own preferred platform as a condition of coaching.

What financial, operational, marketing, or performance information should I provide?

Whatever's relevant to the area you're working on — job costing details for pricing work, current hiring process for team-building work, or full financials for the executive tier's financial review.

What happens if I do not complete an agreed action item?

Real accountability is part of the structure — group calls provide peer accountability, and 1:1 tiers include direct access to your coach specifically to keep action items from slipping.

Group coaching or 1:1 — which should I start with?

Group works well if you're under $1M in revenue and want structured curriculum plus peer accountability. 1:1 makes more sense above $1M when your problems become specific to your company and you need custom answers, not general frameworks.

Will the coaching program be customized for my goals and industry?

Yes — 100% trades-focused rather than generic business coaching, and the tier (group vs. 1:1 vs. executive) is matched to your revenue stage and how specific your problems have become.

Can coaching focus on marketing, operations, leadership, growth, or accountability?

Yes — the three core areas (sales/pricing/margin, hiring/HR/team building, operations/SOPs/growth planning) cover all of those, with emphasis set by what you need most.

Can coaching be combined with Toughjobs implementation services?

Yes — coaching identifies what needs to change; other Toughjobs services (software setup, marketing, automation) can implement it.

Results & Expectations

How long until I see ROI?

Pricing and sales work usually pays back inside 60–90 days. Hiring and systems work is a longer arc — six to twelve months to see the full compounding effect. If a coach promises overnight results, they're selling something else.
