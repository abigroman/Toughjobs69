Coaching

I'm too busy to add coaching calls to my week — is this really the right time?

The reason you're too busy is exactly why you need it. Coaching creates the two hours a month that build the systems that give you back ten hours a week. If you wait until you're "less busy," you'll be having this same conversation in three years.

Group coaching or 1:1 — which should I start with?

Group works well if you're under $1M in revenue and want structured curriculum plus peer accountability. 1:1 makes more sense above $1M when your problems become specific to your company and you need custom answers, not general frameworks.

How long until I see ROI?

Pricing and sales work usually pays back inside 60–90 days. Hiring and systems work is a longer arc — six to twelve months to see the full compounding effect. If a coach promises overnight results, they're selling something else.

Do I have to change my software or vendors to work with a coach?

No. A good coach works with what you have. If your systems genuinely need to change, they'll tell you why and let you decide — they won't sell you their own preferred platform as a condition of coaching.

What if I've already been in business 20 years?

Some of the best coaching results happen with veteran owners who never formalized their systems because everything's always been in their head. A coach helps you document, delegate, and eventually exit — whether "exit" means sale, succession, or just finally taking a real vacation.

LLC Filing

Is Toughjobs a law firm?

No. Toughjobs is not a law firm and does not provide legal, tax, or accounting advice. We provide administrative filing assistance — we help you organize your information, prepare your formation paperwork based on what you give us, and submit it to the state.

What does the price actually include?

Each price is your starting total for the initial LLC formation filing — Illinois is $195, Iowa is $65. Expedited Illinois processing is an optional add-on for an extra $100. We don't mark up the state's own fees.

How much does it cost to form an LLC in Illinois?

The Illinois state filing fee is $150, and our standard total is $195. Expedited Illinois processing is optional and adds a $100 fee, bringing the total to $325 if you choose it. Expedited handling may require in-person filing and availability can vary.

How much does it cost to form an LLC in Iowa?

The Iowa state filing fee is $50, and our total is $65. Iowa is one of the most affordable states in the country to form an LLC.

Are there ongoing fees after I file?

Yes. Illinois requires a $75 annual report fee, and Iowa requires a $30 biennial report fee (filed online every two years) to keep your LLC in good standing. These are separate, recurring state fees — not part of your initial filing total.

Why do prices say "starting"?

State fees are set by the state and can change at any time. We always confirm the current, final price with you before anything is filed, so there are no surprises.

How long does it take?

Timelines are set by the state, not by us. Iowa online filings are often approved within a business day or two; Illinois standard processing takes longer, and expedited is faster. We'll give you the current estimate when you start.

Do I need an attorney instead?

For a simple, straightforward LLC, most people don't. But if you have multiple owners with complex agreements, unusual tax questions, or legal risk, you should consult a licensed attorney or CPA — and we'll be honest with you if that's the better path.

Does forming an LLC protect me from lawsuits or save me on taxes?

We can't promise that. Forming an LLC does not guarantee liability protection, tax savings, loan approval, or business success. Those depend on how your business is run and your specific situation — questions for a licensed professional.

What's a registered agent, and do I need one?

A registered agent is a person or company with a physical address in your state who receives official mail for your LLC. Both Illinois and Iowa require one. You can often be your own, or we can help you coordinate a service as an add-on.

Can you also do my EIN, website, and branding?

Yes — those are optional add-ons priced separately. Once your LLC is filed, we can help with EIN setup, an operating agreement template, a website, logo and branding, a Google Business Profile, business email, and bookkeeping setup.

Who's responsible if my information is wrong?

You are. We file based on the details you provide, so it's important that your business name, addresses, and member information are accurate. We check your name against state records, but the underlying details are your responsibility.

Can you help if I'm not in Illinois or Iowa?

Right now our filing assistance covers Illinois and Iowa. If you're forming in another state, reach out and we'll point you in the right direction.

Contractor Licensing

Do I need more than an LLC to run a contractor business in Illinois?

Usually yes. An LLC registers your business entity with the state, but most trades also need state or local licensing, city registration, permits, and insurance before performing or advertising work.

Do Iowa contractors need to register with the state?

Yes. Iowa requires individuals and businesses earning $2,000 or more per year from construction work to register as a Construction Contractor with the Iowa Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing (DIAL).

Do plumbers need a license in Illinois and Iowa?

Yes in both states. Illinois licenses plumbers statewide through the Illinois Department of Public Health. Iowa licenses plumbing and mechanical contractors through the Iowa Plumbing and Mechanical Systems Board.

Does Illinois have a general contractor license?

No. Illinois does not issue a statewide general contractor license. General contracting, electrical, and HVAC work are regulated at the city or county level, such as Chicago's Department of Buildings.

Do landscapers need a license to apply pesticides?

Typically yes. Applying pesticides or herbicides for hire generally requires a commercial pesticide applicator license through the state department of agriculture in both Illinois and Iowa.

Do roofers need a license in Illinois?

Yes. Illinois requires a statewide roofing contractor license through the Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation under the Roofing Industry Licensing Act.

Do electricians need a state license in Iowa?

Yes. Iowa licenses electrical contractors and electricians through the Iowa Electrical Examining Board, separate from general construction contractor registration.

Can I advertise contractor services before getting licensed?

You should hold any required license or registration before advertising services that legally require one. Advertising services you're not licensed to perform can create legal and reputational risk.

Should I put my license number on my website?

If you hold a license, an accurate license number can build trust. Never display a license number you don't currently hold, or one that doesn't cover the specific work being advertised.

Do city permits matter if I already have an LLC?

Yes. An LLC is a business entity registration and does not replace city or county permits, inspections, or local contractor registration required for specific projects.

What insurance should a home service contractor carry?

Most contractors carry general liability insurance, workers' compensation if they have employees, commercial auto coverage, and sometimes a license or contractor bond depending on the trade and jurisdiction.

Can Toughjobs help me build a compliant contractor website?

Yes. Toughjobs helps contractors build websites, SEO pages, and marketing materials that present licensing, service area, and guarantees accurately. Toughjobs is not a law firm and does not determine licensing requirements for you.
