Branding

Do I really need a brand if I get most of my work from referrals?

Referrals still Google you before they call. A weak or inconsistent brand makes a referred lead second-guess the recommendation, and it caps how much you can charge. Strong branding is what lets you raise prices without losing the referral pipeline.

Can't I just have someone on Fiverr make a logo?

You can, and a lot of contractors do — then they spend the next three years paying wrap shops and printers to fix files that don't work at scale or don't come with the source formats they need. Cheap logos are expensive by year two.

How long does a full brand identity project take?

Four to eight weeks for a standard identity package, longer if vehicle wraps and a website rebuild are in scope. The bottleneck is usually decision-making on your end, not design time.

Should every truck in my fleet look identical?

Yes. Fleet consistency is how you multiply impressions — a homeowner who sees three of your trucks around town this week assumes you're the biggest outfit in the region, even if you only run five vehicles.

What if I already have a logo but everything else is a mess?

That's the most common situation. We can build the color system, typography, standards doc, and applied templates around your existing mark — or clean up the logo first if it's holding the rest back. We'll tell you straight which one you need.

Print Collateral

Does direct mail still work in 2026?

Yes, and better than it did five years ago because so few businesses use it anymore. Response rates on well-targeted trade postcards typically run 0.5–2%, which crushes most digital channels on a per-lead-cost basis. The key is targeting — blanket mailing entire zip codes is wasteful. Saturating carrier routes around completed jobs is not.

How do I make print not look cheap?

Three things. Heavy stock (16pt minimum for cards, 14pt for hangers), full-bleed professional design instead of a Word document, and consistent branding across every piece. Cheap print screams cheap contractor, and that impression sticks.

What's the best neighborhood strategy after a big job?

We call it the "circle strategy." Yard sign in the customer's yard for two weeks. Door hangers on 20 neighbors the day the job wraps. EDDM postcard hits the surrounding two carrier routes 10 days later. Every touchpoint references the completed work. That's how one job becomes five.

How do I track if print is actually working?

Use a dedicated tracking phone number on each print campaign (not your main line). QR codes with UTM parameters if you're driving to a landing page. Ask every incoming caller "how'd you hear about us?" and log it in the CRM. Within 90 days you'll know exactly what's producing.

What's the biggest print mistake contractors make?

Cluttered design. Trying to list every service, every certification, every discount on one card. The best-performing pieces have one message, one call to action, one phone number, and a lot of white space. Say less, sell more.

Video Creation & Editing

How fast is your turnaround on video edits?

Starter carries a 48-hour SLA on standard cuts. Growth is 24–36 hours and Enterprise Scale is priority same-day to 24 hours. Complex motion graphics, custom animation, or a multi-module onboarding suite go on a build calendar you approve before work starts — so nothing is ever “in progress” without a date attached.

How do I get raw footage to you?

We set up a shared drive folder on day one. Upload straight from camera, phone, Zoom, Loom, or your existing cloud storage. We accept ProRes, H.264, H.265, MP4, MOV, and screen recordings, and we handle proxies and transcoding on our side. If your files are on SD cards, mail them — we return the cards.

Is there a cap on revisions?

Starter and Growth include two revision rounds per deliverable, which covers the overwhelming majority of projects when the brief is filled out. Enterprise Scale is unlimited inside your monthly hour pool. Anything past the included rounds draws from retainer hours at your published rate — never a surprise line item.

Who handles commercial music licensing?

We do, and it is included. Every delivered edit is scored with music cleared for commercial and paid-media use, and the license documentation ships with your final files. We will not hand you a video with a track that can get an ad account restricted or a channel claimed — even if you ask for a song we cannot clear.

What happens to hours I don’t use?

Up to 50% of your monthly hours roll into the next month and stay live for 30 days. That is designed for real business rhythms — a quiet February funds a heavy March product launch. Overages bill at $75–$125/hr by tier, always flagged for approval before we spend them.

Do you shoot footage, or only edit?

The retainer is built around editing and post — that is where the volume and the margin are for you. We do run on-site shoot days across Peoria, the Quad Cities, and Dubuque as a separate line item, and we will coach your team on capturing usable footage with the phone already in their pocket so shoot days stay optional.
