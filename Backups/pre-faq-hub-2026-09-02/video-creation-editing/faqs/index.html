Timeline & Completion

How fast is your turnaround on video edits?

Starter carries a 48-hour SLA on standard cuts. Growth is 24–36 hours and Enterprise Scale is priority same-day to 24 hours. Complex motion graphics, custom animation, or a multi-module onboarding suite go on a build calendar you approve before work starts — so nothing is ever “in progress” without a date attached.

What's Included

Who handles commercial music licensing?

We do, and it is included. Every delivered edit is scored with music cleared for commercial and paid-media use, and the license documentation ships with your final files. We will not hand you a video with a track that can get an ad account restricted or a channel claimed — even if you ask for a song we cannot clear.

Do you shoot footage, or only edit?

The retainer is built around editing and post — that is where the volume and the margin are for you. We do run on-site shoot days across Peoria, the Quad Cities, and Dubuque as a separate line item, and we will coach your team on capturing usable footage with the phone already in their pocket so shoot days stay optional.

Requirements & Preparation

How do I get raw footage to you?

We set up a shared drive folder on day one. Upload straight from camera, phone, Zoom, Loom, or your existing cloud storage. We accept ProRes, H.264, H.265, MP4, MOV, and screen recordings, and we handle proxies and transcoding on our side. If your files are on SD cards, mail them — we return the cards.

Revisions & Changes

Is there a cap on revisions?

Starter and Growth include two revision rounds per deliverable, which covers the overwhelming majority of projects when the brief is filled out. Enterprise Scale is unlimited inside your monthly hour pool. Anything past the included rounds draws from retainer hours at your published rate — never a surprise line item.

Policies & Guarantees

What happens to hours I don’t use?

Up to 50% of your monthly hours roll into the next month and stay live for 30 days. That is designed for real business rhythms — a quiet February funds a heavy March product launch. Overages bill at $75–$125/hr by tier, always flagged for approval before we spend them.
