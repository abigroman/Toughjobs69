What's Included

What exactly does the Toughjobs partnership program provide?

You bring the truck, tools, and skill. Toughjobs hands you the website, brand, systems, and marketing needed to turn one truck into a real company — the full operation, not just a logo.

Who It's For

Who is the partnership program actually for?

Tradespeople ready to go independent who already have the skill and equipment but not the marketing, brand, or business systems to book consistent work on their own.

Requirements & Preparation

Do I need my own truck and tools to apply?

Yes. The program is built around what you already bring to the table — your truck, tools, and trade skill. Toughjobs supplies the business side: website, brand, systems, and marketing.

Customization & Options

What's the difference between the partnership track and Helping Hands?

The partnership track hands you a full operation — website, brand, systems, and marketing. Helping Hands is a one-day-a-week program for people with more grit than budget: honest guidance and real direction, no pressure, without the full build-out.

Contact & Next Steps

How do I get involved or apply?

Reach out through the contact page with your trade and current setup. From there it's a conversation about fit — no pressure, no obligation to commit on the first call.
