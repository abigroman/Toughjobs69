Pricing & Costs

Are these tools actually free, or is there a catch?

They're free to use, no card required. Each one is built to show you a real, specific problem in your marketing — a ranking gap, a slow page, wasted ad spend — so you can decide for yourself whether it's worth fixing. There's no obligation to hire Toughjobs afterward.

What's Included

What does the SEO FAQ & schema generator actually produce?

Enter your trade and target keyword and it builds a ready-to-publish Q&A section for your site plus valid FAQPage schema markup, so the same content that helps a homeowner also helps Google understand your page.

Requirements & Preparation

Do I need to know my numbers before using the marketing plan builder?

No. It's built to work from what you already know about your business — service area, current channels, rough budget — and turns that into a visual plan you can act on or bring to a strategy call.

Customization & Options

How is the Competitor Snapshot different from the Full Deep-Dive Audit?

The free snapshot gives you an instant, high-level look at how you stack up against nearby competitors. The paid Full Deep-Dive Audit goes much deeper — verified rankings on 50+ keywords, a site architecture audit, review and reputation analysis, paid ad intelligence, backlink gaps, and a custom 90-day roadmap.
