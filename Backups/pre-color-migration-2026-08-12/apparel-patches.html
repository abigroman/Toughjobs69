Custom Patches for trade crews

Premium custom patches for shirts, jackets and work uniforms. Iron-on and sew-on, built for workwear.

Ordering custom patches for a crew involves more than placing a logo on a blank item. Toughjobs helps define who will wear it, where it will be used, which decoration method fits the material, and what must remain readable after regular work and washing. The order is not released until the product, color, sizes, artwork placement, quantity, and production proof are confirmed.

Product and order details

  • Reference product: STAHLS custom embroidered / FlexStyle
  • Material: Embroidered, faux leather, dimensional and other finishes — quoted per design
  • Available directions: Embroidered Twill, Woven, PVC / Rubber
  • Planning range: $1.74 – $12 each; final price is confirmed after quantity, artwork, placement, and stock are reviewed.

Stock and color availability can change. A quoted option is treated as a starting point until the supplier confirms inventory. If the requested item is unavailable, the replacement should be approved for material, fit, color, and price before production begins.

Care guidance

  • Patches are pre-finished — follow the care instructions of the garment they're applied to.
  • Iron-on patches: do not dry clean.
  • Machine wash cold, inside out.

What to send for an accurate quote

Send the approximate quantity, size breakdown, preferred colors, in-hands date, delivery location, and the best available logo file. Vector artwork is ideal, but the team can review what you have. Note every placement you need—such as left chest, back, sleeve, hat front, or patch location—because each placement affects the proof and final scope.
