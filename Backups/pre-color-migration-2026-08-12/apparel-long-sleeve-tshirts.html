Long-Sleeve T-Shirts for trade crews

Comfortable branded layers for cooler jobs and changing weather. All-season crew comfort.

Ordering long-sleeve t-shirts for a crew involves more than placing a logo on a blank item. Toughjobs helps define who will wear it, where it will be used, which decoration method fits the material, and what must remain readable after regular work and washing. The order is not released until the product, color, sizes, artwork placement, quantity, and production proof are confirmed.

Product and order details

  • Reference product: Gildan Ultra Cotton 2400 / G240
  • Material: ≈ 6 oz cotton — some colors are blends
  • Available directions: 100% Cotton, Cotton/Poly Blend, Performance
  • Planning range: $8 – $38 each; final price is confirmed after quantity, artwork, placement, and stock are reviewed.

Stock and color availability can change. A quoted option is treated as a starting point until the supplier confirms inventory. If the requested item is unavailable, the replacement should be approved for material, fit, color, and price before production begins.

Care guidance

  • Machine wash cold, inside out, with like colors.
  • Tumble dry low.
  • Do not iron directly over print or embroidery.
  • Do not bleach.

What to send for an accurate quote

Send the approximate quantity, size breakdown, preferred colors, in-hands date, delivery location, and the best available logo file. Vector artwork is ideal, but the team can review what you have. Note every placement you need—such as left chest, back, sleeve, hat front, or patch location—because each placement affects the proof and final scope.
